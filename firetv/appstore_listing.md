## 🌟 Long Description
**MacroPulse** brings hedge-fund-grade quantitative macroeconomic intelligence directly to the living room and trading desk big screen. Designed specifically for Amazon Fire TV, MacroPulse allows active investors and portfolio managers to track real-time macroeconomic stress regimes, monitor Treasury yield curve slopes, simulate multi-asset portfolio drawdowns, and run instant FOMC rate decision shock tests.

### Key Features:
1. **10-Foot Big Screen Desk**: Designed from the ground up for high-contrast viewing from your couch or trading wall display with oversized typography and overscan-safe margins.
2. **Cross-Asset FOMC Rate Shock Engine**: Connect read-only brokerage portfolios (Alpaca, Interactive Brokers, Plaid schema) or choose institutional presets to stress test multi-asset portfolios against +50 bps rate hikes, dovish pivots, and stagflationary shocks in real time.
3. **Alexa Voice Integration**: Seamlessly switch views and query metrics using hands-free voice commands:
   * *"Alexa, ask MacroPulse for today's market regime"*
   * *"Alexa, show the NVDA Danger Zone on the TV"*
   * *"Alexa, run an FOMC rate shock test"*
4. **Personalized Ambient Audio Chimes**: Subtle, soothing procedural audio cues played through your TV soundbar when high-probability Bollinger Squeeze breakouts or credit spread divergences occur.
5. **NVIDIA Danger Zone Monitor**: Track composite microstructure risk, block trades, and order-flow imbalance before market open.
