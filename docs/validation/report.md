## Reading the results

**H1, regime: significant effect.** After a Risk-Off month end the next month's realised volatility is about 1.9x that after a Risk-On month end, in every sub-sample and setting tried. Limits: the score includes trailing 12-month realised volatility, so part of this is volatility clustering; the credit spread's own contribution was not isolated (not pre-registered). The score is not a recession signal: it turns Risk-Off after recessions begin, and most Risk-Off months are outside recessions. The next-month return is not lower after Risk-Off (two-sided p 0.087).

**H2, expected return: not decidable under the pre-registered rule.** The pipeline check missed the 0.5 pp tolerance by 0.09 pp on one of six primary values (d/p, forecasts from 1965); the other five are within it. It needed two fixes after the first run, both to match the paper's own procedure: pre-1926 returns (absent from the current data file) and the paper's adjusted OOS R² (Equation 6; confirmed by its cay3 example). Descriptively the model loses to the prevailing mean: OOS R² -45.8% since 1985 and -29.4% on the product's 2005 start, worse than the average model fitted to shuffled returns (-33.1%). The leakage checks are clean. Until a pipeline fix passes the check, the product should not describe this forecast as informative.

**H3, strategy: no effect.** The Sharpe ratio is above zero on its own (PSR 0.97), but not above the best of 32 rules expected by chance (DSR 0.61 < 0.95), and SPA finds no win over buy-and-hold, the 10-month trend rule or 60/40. Low PBO over 1960-2026 means the choice among the 32 rules is not what failed; over 2005-2026 PBO is 0.66.

**H4, VaR: 95% calibrated; 99% under-covers.** The 99% VaR was exceeded 9 times against 3.9 expected (Kupiec p 0.027, Basel yellow), and the expected shortfall is too small (Z2 p 0.013): the normal model's tails are too thin. The bootstrap of daily returns does slightly better and is still yellow.

**H5, DCF: no effect.** The mean yearly rank IC is -0.020 and the ratings do not order next-year excess returns. Limits: survivorship (companies that no longer map to an SEC CIK or Yahoo ticker drop out), a community constituent list, and the regime and market drift taken from today's data vintage. 398 of 2173 name-years give an upside above +500% or below -95%: negative fair values when FCFF is negative (airlines in 2020-21, utilities, AMZN 2021-22) and a share count far too small for FOX/FOXA. These are engine issues to fix in a new, separately scored model version.

**Data.** BAA/AAA are published a median 3 days after month end, so the product's regime, which puts month m's Moody's average in row m, sees it a few days early. Using the values known at each month end changes 5% of labels and the OOS R² by under 1 pp. CPI, unemployment and industrial production are revised often; that affects only the offline research pipeline (src/data_fred.py), which uses today's vintage.
